# THE RECEIPT — Product Definition

## Product promise

**Put it on the record.** THE RECEIPT lets people make human predictions, attach confidence and a resolution date, lock the statement permanently, and return later to discover whether they were right.

## Who it is for

People who enjoy internet culture, friendly arguments, sports-style prediction, journaling, collecting evidence of their past opinions, and the emotional payoff of being proven right—or being hilariously wrong.

## Core loop

Discover a question → make a prediction → set confidence → lock it → receive a receipt → wait → resolve → see whether you were right → share or compare → make another prediction.

## Current product surface

The MVP supports a daily global prompt, custom predictions, confidence, future resolution dates, public/private visibility, shareable receipt detail, owner resolution, archive filters, profiles, challenges, and a demo leaderboard. The application is designed mobile-first and aims for a first daily receipt in under 30 seconds.

## Product principles

1. The prediction is the star.
2. The receipt is the product object.
3. The daily challenge creates a reason to return.
4. Public receipts create a reason to share.
5. Challenges create social competition.
6. Resolution creates anticipation.
7. History creates identity.
8. Accuracy matters, but being wrong should remain entertaining.
9. The core experience does not require AI.
10. Every added feature must strengthen discovery, prediction, anticipation, resolution, identity, competition, or sharing.

## Safety and boundaries

This is not a gambling product. The current MVP includes no money, betting, crypto, prizes, financial wagering, political prediction category, medical advice, or financial advice.

## Success signals

The most important future measurements are landing-to-signup conversion, signup-to-first-receipt conversion, first receipt completion, daily participation, receipts created per user, resolution rate, share clicks, shared receipt to new signup, challenge creation/acceptance, D1 retention, D7 retention, and D30 retention.
